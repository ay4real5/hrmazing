Privacy Policy

Draft — needs review before launch. This page was written as a starting point and has not been checked by anyone qualified. Confirm the details are accurate for the business, and have it reviewed before taking real orders.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, contact details, delivery address and what you ordered, including any engraving wording or gift message you supply.

Payment details

We never see or store your card details. Payments are processed by Stripe, and card information is entered on Stripe’s own systems. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

How we use it

Only to take, make and deliver your order, and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without you asking.

Gifts sent without an address

If you choose to send a gift without knowing the recipient’s address, we use the contact detail you provide solely to ask them for delivery details, and for nothing else afterwards.

Stored in your browser

Your basket is kept in your own browser’s local storage so it is still there if you come back. It never leaves your device until you check out. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it. Contact hermazingtouch@gmail.com.
